A studio named after a lighthouse

Pharosline is a digital creative agency. We make motion graphic videos, social reels, advertising campaigns, brand identities and documents, and we work in both Arabic and English.

Our own brand film sums up how we think: start with the noise, find the idea, give it a strong look, then send it straight to the people it's for.

brand intro

Client
Pharosline (in-house)
Deliverables
Logo mark, key visual, 8-scene motion film
Format
16:9, 1280×720, about 42 seconds

Challenge

Introduce a brand-new agency and everything it makes — videos, ads, websites, design and content plans — in one short film, without it feeling like a list.

Strategy

Tell it as a story the audience already lives: a phone full of noise, a single idea that lights up, the craft that shapes it, and the moment it reaches people.

Creative direction

Halftone photo cut-outs, torn paper, masking tape and one amber brush stroke — set on warm paper, framed by an editing timeline that keeps the film's own making visible.

Execution, scene by scene

Brand applications

The mark — a heavy P with an amber block as its lamp — carries from the logo into every frame of the film, the key visual and this website.
